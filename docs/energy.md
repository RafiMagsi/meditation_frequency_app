Who gave you the mind and the thought power? The god himself.

Let us understand the basic concepts.

The soul that you have is a part of the god himself. The god is infinite. Such infinity, that if you take one infinite from it, still an infinite is left. It is like lighting a candle with another. You take the light from one candle to light up another, but the light in the first candle still remains. When, at the origin of life, god disintegrated, the smaller fragments of god became soul and the soul then resided in the physical bodies. So, my soul, and your soul, were once together in one entity (Equality of all plants, animals, and humans comes from this reason).

Now, the soul that you have run your brain. The food that you eat provides you with thought power. The sub-conscious mind is your soul, the part of infinite intelligence. It is the part of god himself. If you believe in god, you should also believe in your sub-conscious thoughts, and thusly, you should treat the ideas of your mind power or thought power as the orders of god himself.
So, now you can see that your mind power and the god himself are basically the same. People, because they cannot understand this, have created an image outside and call it god. Ever wonder why so many people ask us to seek god within ourselves first? Because god is within ourselves, in all of us (God helps those, who help themselves. Getting a picture now?)

If you trust in yourself, you trust in your god. This is the ultimate truth.

How to be one with your god? Listening to your inner voice. You can call it your mind power, your subconsious calling whatever. It is the same thing. How can you listen to your inner desires and calling you may ask. To see our innerself, the beautiful metaphor used by Vivekananda is that our being is like a lake. And the inner self is at the bottom of that lake. Now you cannot see the inner self because there is a lot of mud and ripples in the lake. Start by clearing out the mud (negative thoughts, greed, hatred, ego, etc.). Then remove the ripples and agitation in the water (calm down yourself, be in a state of thoughtlessness). Now with the ripples and the mud gone, you would see your true innerself. This inner self is a part of the god himself. Listen to the orders of this inner self and do your actions in this world. This inner self and the god are not different.

My suggestion is: Stop thinking of god as an external entity. Seek god within you, god has you, and you have god. Your mind thoughts and god are in no way different. There is no god in any image outside. The image was made by the humans who could not understand the infinity of god. Image is only symbolic. See the god as infinite, and his infinite fiber going through you. Trust in yourself and in your particular god. Do not fear as your god is within you and for you.
